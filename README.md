
 # 👨‍💻 About dailycodepython


This repository serves as a practical log for an independent, amateur exploration of advanced mathematical methods and cloud/data workflows. 

The core process relies on **AI-Intensive Math Self-Education** and **Intelligent Modeling**—utilizing artificial intelligence as the primary navigator to bridge the gap in formal advanced mathematical background.

### 🔬 Methodology & Context

All calculations, algorithms, and visualizations are developed in close collaboration with AI assistants. This approach has distinct characteristics that define the nature of the projects:

*   **Advantages:** Enables an immediate transition from high-level abstract mathematical concepts directly into working Python code, bypassing multi-year academic theoretical cycles.
*   **Limitations:** Carries an inherent risk of missing foundational prerequisites, lacks formal academic verification, and relies on the correctness of AI interpretations in complex theoretical domains.

*Everything here is provided "as is" as a study of how a modern enthusiast can interact with high-end disciplines in symbiosis with technology.*

---

### 🚀 Key Areas of Exploration

*   **Intelligent Mathematical Modeling:** Practical implementation of Quantum Computing basics, Topological Data Analysis (TDA), and Bayesian Statistics.
*   **Game Theory & Decision Frameworks:** Stochastic optimization models, optimal transport (Wasserstein Distance), and Bellman's Backwards Induction.
*   **Applied Analytics:** Predictive modeling and data quality workflows leveraging frameworks like PyCaret.
